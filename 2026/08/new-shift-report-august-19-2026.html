New Shift Report: August 19, 2026

A new edition of The Shift Report is live, with a focused roundup of timely information for sleep technologists.

This update highlights the Sleep Professionals of Alabama Annual Sleep Conference, including a speaker shout-out to Byron Jamerson, RPSGT, RST, CCSH, FAAST, along with the AASM Foundation travel grant for sleep technologists and advanced practice providers.

It also includes current AAST member-free CEC opportunities, 2027 home sleep testing coding updates, a sleep-specific coding education deadline, factual Inspire recall information linked to FDA material, and a current BRPT board opportunity.

There is also a hopeful look ahead to Southern Sleep Society 2027, with a special recognition of Marietta B. Bibbs, BA, RPSGT, CCSH, FAAST, FAASM.

What we are keeping out: routine reminders, unchanged policies, and update dates that do not represent an actual change. The goal is to keep The Shift Report focused on information that is new, newly available, changing, approaching a deadline, or directly useful to working sleep professionals.
