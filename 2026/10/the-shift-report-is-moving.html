The Shift Report Is Moving — We're Staying Connected

By Tracy Frazier, RHIT, RPSGT, CCS-P · Founder, Sleep Pathways Guild

Dear fellow sleep technologists, students, and friends of Sleep Pathways Guild,

I'm sharing an important update: we are closing the separate website at shift.sleeppathwaysguild.com and bringing our updates back to the Sleep Pathways Guild blog and Facebook community.

The Shift Report was created to make sleep-medicine news, professional developments, research highlights, career information, and educational opportunities easier to find. That purpose remains important. What is changing is where we gather and share it.

Where to find us now

Our blog at blog.sleeppathwaysguild.com will be our central home for articles, announcements, professional updates, sleep-technology study resources, and future Shift-style coverage.

Our Facebook page will be our place to stay connected as a community, share new posts, offer encouragement, and hear from sleep professionals and learners. Please follow the page and become part of our Guild community, and invite colleagues and students who may benefit.

Read the Guild Blog →Join Us on Facebook →
Please update your bookmarks: Instead of visiting shift.sleeppathwaysguild.com, head to blog.sleeppathwaysguild.com for new content.

What is not changing

Sleep Pathways Guild is not closing. Our commitment to free, practical sleep-technology education continues, including support for RPSGT and CPSGT learners, working polysomnographic technologists, and those exploring the profession. Our main Guild website and study tools remain available.

Consolidating our publishing channels will help keep information easier to find and allow more attention to go toward useful educational content and the people who use it. Older Shift Report articles may still be visible during the transition; as always, verify time-sensitive information against the original sources.

Thank you for reading, sharing, learning, and helping build this community. I hope you'll follow along on the blog and Facebook as we continue the work together.

Tracy Frazier, RHIT, RPSGT, CCS-P
