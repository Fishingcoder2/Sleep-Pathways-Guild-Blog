The Free Sleep Pathways Guild CPSGT Study App Has Been Released

A new self-guided learning pathway for CPSGT candidates, students, and entry-level sleep-technology learners.

Sleep Pathways Guild has released the free CPSGT Study Launchpad. The webapp is designed to help learners organize CPSGT review around the three exam domains, all eight official task areas, repeated practice, and weak-area repair.

Launch the Free CPSGT Study App →

No purchase or account is required. Learning activity and personalization are stored in the learner’s own browser.

What is included?

600 practice questionsChoose mixed practice, a domain, an official task, or a focused topic.
75-question mock-style examPractice in untimed mode or use the optional two-hour pacing timer.
Blueprint and task alignmentReview the three CPSGT domains and all eight official task areas.
126 glossary terms and flashcardsBuild terminology, recall, and task-level connections.
Math CoachWork through 13 calculation lessons, formulas, examples, and practice.
Memory and equipment labsReview abbreviations, formulas, sensors, measurements, signal checks, and common artifacts.
Missed-question repairSee the best answer, reasoning, Coach Bob guidance, and the next study move.
Personalized progress reportReview domain and task performance, study priorities, question history, and Guided Trail progress.

Built for self-guided study

The Launchpad combines practice, targeted review, mock-style testing, sticky-note flashcards, a CPSGT glossary, Math Coach, an acronym-and-formula memory lab, equipment and measurement review, Guided Trail badges, and a personalized learner report in one browser-based app.

The app also includes APA-cited optional learning resources and a separate Guild Resource Shelf. Books are optional companions—not requirements for using the app—and affiliate relationships do not determine the educational criteria used to include a title.

How to begin

  1. Open the CPSGT Study Launchpad.
  2. Read the “How to Use This App” section.
  3. Start with mixed practice or select the domain or task that needs the most attention.
  4. Use the missed-question queue instead of only repeating familiar questions.
  5. Review the progress report and use its task-specific study priorities.
