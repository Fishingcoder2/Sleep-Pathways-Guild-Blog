Good morning, artifact-hunters and weekend warriors. It is Saturday, June 13. You made it to the weekend, and for those of you in Baltimore, welcome to ground zero. The APSS SLEEP 2026 Trainee Symposia and Fellow Boot Camp. Yesterday we touched on the incoming wave of home testing and smartphone apps. Today, we are looking at the legal realities of that AI tech, celebrating a big credentialing win, and strategizing your weekend hustle. Let's get into it.

🤖 Clinical Science & Tech: The AI Reality Check

We spent a lot of time this week discussing AI as a revolutionary diagnostic tool. But the honeymoon phase is over.

On Monday, Dr. Aatif Husain is leading a highly anticipated session at SLEEP 2026 focused entirely on the pitfalls of artificial intelligence in sleep medicine. They are tackling the global, legal, and training liabilities that could compromise data integrity and patient safety when AI is deployed carelessly.

The Reality Check: Algorithms are only as good as the raw data feeding them, and they do not have a license to lose. As AI increasingly encroaches on auto-scoring and preliminary diagnostics, your clinical intuition is the ultimate firewall. When the software confidently labels a 3-minute artifact storm as REM sleep, it is on you to correct it. Do not let an algorithm bully you into signing off on garbage data.

📜 Credentialing & Education: A BRPT Win

Let’s pause the conference hype for some excellent homegrown news. Yesterday, the BRPT officially announced the winner of the 2026 A-STEP/STAR Educational Grant.

Huge congratulations to Sierra Redd, who just secured the grant toward her educational program to become a fully credentialed RPSGT. Earning this credential is a grind, and financial barriers stop too many talented trainees from crossing the finish line.

If you are a Lead Tech or a lab manager reading this, take note. Support your un-credentialed night staff. Mentor them, help them prep for the boards, and make sure they know when these BRPT grants open up. We need more sharp techs in the trenches.

🎓 Free CEUs: The Farming Season Begins

For those of you not wandering the Inner Harbor in Baltimore this weekend, your job is to keep a close eye on your browser.

While the live general sessions start tomorrow, today’s educational boot camps mean the information dam is about to break. Watch the AASM and Sleep Research Society learning portals closely over the next 48 hours. As the major sessions conclude, sponsors and organizations will begin posting summary webinars and post-conference quizzes. Have your BRPT ID number handy and be ready to snatch up those freshly minted CEUs.

💼 RPSGT Job Board: Face-to-Face Leverage

If you are physically at SLEEP 2026 this weekend, remember that the conference floor is the most lucrative job board in the country.

  • Bypass the HR Black Hole: Lab directors and hospital network VPs are wandering the same halls you are. If you are looking to relocate, step into a management role, or pivot into clinical research, this is your moment. Have your resume ready, ditch the online application portals, and hand your credentials directly to the decision-makers at the networking mixers.
  • The Weekend Shift: For the rest of you holding down the fort in the lab tonight, remember that weekend shift differentials are your best friend. Keep the coffee strong, and don't be afraid to ask for premium PRN rates if they are begging you to cover a Sunday night call-out.

Check your calibrations, trust your eyes over the algorithm, and have a solid weekend. We will see you on the next shift.
