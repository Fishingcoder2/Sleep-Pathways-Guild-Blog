⭐ Top Reads This Week

Authority Watch – Sleep Medicine Guidelines & Compliance
Highlights from professional organizations and regulatory bodies that impact sleep study protocols, PAP titration policies, scoring rules, and lab accreditation. Short, practical summaries designed for working RPSGTs and night‑shift techs.

Research Watch – New Sleep Research for Technologists
New studies in sleep-disordered breathing, insomnia, PAP adherence, and diagnostic testing, broken down into what matters at the bedside and in the control room. Ideal for RPSGT exam prep, CCSH exam prep, and ongoing continuing education.

Career Corner – Sleep Technologist Jobs & Career Trends
Signals from the sleep technologist job market, including RPSGT salary trends, remote scoring opportunities, HSAT positions, and lab management roles. Useful for techs planning their next step in sleep medicine careers.

Vendor Watch – Sleep Lab Equipment & Software Updates
Changes in polysomnography systems, PAP devices, HSAT equipment, and scoring software that may affect nightly workflow, troubleshooting, and signal quality. Perfect for techs who like to stay ahead of hardware and software updates.
