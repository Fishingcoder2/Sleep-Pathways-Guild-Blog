🌙 This Week on The Shift Report

The Shift Report at shift.sleeppathwaysguild.com continues to evolve into a clean, minimalist weekly briefing for working sleep technologists. Designed for a single scroll and a single breath, it delivers the essentials without the noise.

“The Shift Report is the weekly pulse of the profession — quiet, steady, and essential.”

What’s Featured This Week

  • Clinical Headlines: PAP adherence trends, insomnia guideline updates, and new circadian research.
  • Credentialing Notes: RPSGT and CCSH study reminders, plus lab competency updates.
  • Education Tools: Case‑based learning, Guild study links, and mentoring pathways.
  • Guild Updates: Conference coverage, technologist awards, and new book releases.
  • Quick Links: AASM, SRS, AAST, BRPT, and Guild resources.
Shift Report Philosophy:
Fast. Scannable. Useful. A weekly companion for night techs, students, and CCSH clinicians.

🎤 Inside SLEEP 2026 in Baltimore

This year’s SLEEP conference brought together the full ecosystem of sleep medicine — clinicians, technologists, researchers, educators, and innovators. Baltimore became a crossroads of ideas, data, and the future of care.

For technologists, SLEEP 2026 offered a rare look at how research becomes practice, how guidelines evolve, and how the profession continues to expand its influence.

🧭 What This Means for the Guild

The Shift Report gives us the weekly rhythm. SLEEP 2026 gives us the yearly momentum. The awards and books highlight the people shaping the profession. Together, they show a field where technologists are not just implementing care — they are shaping the future of sleep medicine.

As the Sleep Pathways Guild grows, the mission remains clear: elevate technologists, strengthen education, and build a community where every level of the profession has a place.
