Artifact study questions

Why should artifact be reviewed before scoring or reporting?

Artifact can change how a signal appears. The Guild’s study materials encourage learners to connect signal quality, physiologic plausibility, channel relationships, and troubleshooting before drawing conclusions.

Does this page replace hands-on clinical training?

No. Artifact recognition requires formal education, supervised experience, current procedures, and professional judgment.

Are the examples copied from an examination?

No. Sleep Pathways Guild states that its practice content is original and does not use recalled, copied, or proprietary examination questions.
