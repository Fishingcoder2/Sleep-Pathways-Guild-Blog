RPSGT study questions

Is the RPSGT webapp free?

Yes. The Guild presents the RPSGT webapp as a free, self-guided educational resource.

Are the practice questions copied from the credentialing examination?

No. Sleep Pathways Guild states that its practice content is original and does not use recalled, copied, or proprietary examination questions.

What should I use for current rules and eligibility?

Use BRPT for current candidate information, eligibility, examination policies, and blueprints. Use current official clinical sources and facility policy for clinical standards and procedures.
