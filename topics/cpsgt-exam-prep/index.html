CPSGT study questions

Is the CPSGT Study Launchpad free?

Yes. No purchase or account is required. Learning progress is stored in the learner’s browser.

Does the app contain recalled examination questions?

No. Sleep Pathways Guild states that its practice questions and explanations are original educational content and do not reproduce recalled or proprietary examination questions.

Does using the app guarantee a passing result?

No. The app is independent educational support and does not predict eligibility, competency, an official score, or a passing result.
